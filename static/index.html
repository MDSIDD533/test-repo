Safety & limitations

No live orders are possible in this version. Signals are rule-based estimates, not guarantees. Research evidence is optional and must never be fabricated; when it is unavailable the pipeline reports UNAVAILABLE and returns NO TRADE. Paper trades are in memory and reset on restart. Persistent storage, AI chat and alert delivery remain future phases.
